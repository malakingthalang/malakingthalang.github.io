Our Story

关于我们 ABOUT US

Founded in 2010, Mala Restaurant brings the authentic flavors of Sichuan province to your table. Our name "Mala" (麻辣) represents the unique combination of spicy (辣) and numbing (麻) sensations that define Sichuan cuisine.

Head Chef Wang, trained in Chengdu for over 20 years, leads our kitchen with passion and expertise. We import our Sichuan peppercorns directly from Hanyuan County and use traditional cooking methods passed down through generations.
